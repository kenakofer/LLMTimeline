Prototype: one box per model, placed at release

Each box is a model, placed at its first public appearance; the bar along its bottom is its Epoch Capabilities Index (ECI) score, and ★ marks a model that topped every earlier model when it launched. Busy years get more horizontal room, so gridline spacing varies. Switch to Capability to lift every model to its score. Arrows show documented lineage (fine-tuned or distilled from). Hover any model to trace its lineage and see its lifespan.
